# RageBabe — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: Oct 12, 2026

RageBabe ("we", "us", or "the app") is a playful stress-relief app. This
policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your
choices. We've tried to keep it plain and honest.

## The short version

- You don't create an account with your name or email. The app uses an
  anonymous ID.
- We store your hit counts, streaks, chosen username, and (if you set one)
  your avatar image, so the app and its leaderboard work.
- We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
- You can request deletion of your data at any time (see "Your choices").

## What we collect

**Anonymous identifier.** When you first open the app, we create an
anonymous account for you through our backend provider (Supabase). This is
a random ID — it is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or Apple
ID. It exists so your hits and streak can be saved and so you can appear on
the leaderboard if you choose to.

**Activity data.** We store your hit counts (daily and all-time) and your
streak information. This is the core of what the app does and what the
leaderboard ranks.

**Username.** If you choose to join the leaderboard, the username you pick
is stored and shown publicly on the leaderboard alongside your hit counts.
Choosing a username is optional — you can use the app without joining.

**Avatar image.** If you choose to set a photo as your bag's face, that
image is stored so it can be shown in the app and on your device's widget.
Choosing an emoji instead keeps everything on your device. You are not
required to add a photo.

## What we do NOT collect

- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or Apple ID.
- We do not collect your contacts, precise location, or health data.
- We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
- We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

## Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase, a cloud backend provider, on their
secured servers. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## Public information

If you join the leaderboard, your **username and hit counts** are visible
to other users of the app. Nothing else about you is shown. If you don't
join, nothing about you is public.

## Children's privacy

RageBabe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided
us data, contact us and we will remove it.

## Your choices

- **Use an emoji instead of a photo** to keep your avatar entirely on your
  device.
- **Don't join the leaderboard** to keep your activity private.
- **Request deletion.** You can ask us to delete your data — your anonymous
  account, counts, streak, username, and avatar — by contacting us at the
  email below. Deleting the app also removes your local data from your
  device.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the
top will reflect any changes.
